### AgeMindBench: Developmentally Grounded Seeker Simulation for Interactive Evaluation of Mental Health Support

Xinjie Zhang, **Zheng Wang**, Sibo Zhao, Ruichen Ma, Xin Wang, Shujie Ren, Wenxuan Wang, and Qin Jin.

**Submitted to ACL Rolling Review, October 2026.** Co-first author and project lead.

### A Survey of LLM-based Agents in Medicine: How far are we from Baymax?

Wenxuan Wang, Zizhan Ma, **Zheng Wang**, Chenghan Wu, Jiaming Ji, Wenting Chen, Xiang Li, and Yixuan Yuan.

**Findings of ACL 2025**, July 2025, pp. 10345–10359. Third-listed author; the first two authors contributed equally.

We review the architectures, applications, and evaluation of LLM-based agents in medicine, and discuss open challenges for their deployment.

[ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.539/) · [PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.539.pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.539)
