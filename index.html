01 / About

Research interests

I am interested in how agents reason, use tools, and recover from failures over long tasks. My work combines agent systems with process-level evaluation and human judgment, with particular interest in healthcare and mental health.

I co-lead AgeMindBench, an interactive evaluation project for mental health support, and contributed to a survey of LLM-based agents in medicine published in Findings of ACL 2025. I have built multi-agent simulation pipelines, expert-evaluation interfaces, and a Python toolkit for measuring semantic diversity.

  • Reliable long-horizon agents
  • Reasoning & tool use
  • Process-level evaluation
  • Failure diagnosis & self-correction
  • Human-facing AI
