About Me

My work sits between empirical NLP research and end-to-end system building. I am interested in how language models reason across people, contexts, and long-running tasks, and how better data, objectives, and evaluation can make tool-using agents more capable and reliable.

I have contributed to two ACL Findings papers on medical agents and age-diverse mental health support. I also have experience building multi-agent simulation systems, expert evaluation platforms, and semantic diversity metrics.

Research Interests

  • LLM reasoning and post-training: data, objectives, and optimization for stronger reasoning.
  • Agent systems: tool-using and multi-agent workflows for planning and collaboration.
  • Model evaluation: reliable, human-centered benchmarks and evaluation methodologies.
  • AI for science: interdisciplinary applications of language models with domain expertise.
